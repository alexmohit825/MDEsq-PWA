Washington State & Federal Standards

Washington Non-Compete Floor RCW 49.62

Non-competes are void in Washington unless earnings exceed statutory thresholds ($120,559+/yr). Max 18-month duration presumption, and moonlighting protections apply. Attempting unlawful enforcement grants the physician mandatory statutory damages + attorney fees.

Stark Law FMV Corridor 42 U.S.C. § 1395nn

Compensation must be consistent with Fair Market Value for clinical services rendered, without regard to volume or value of downstream hospital admissions or surgical referrals.

Malpractice Tail Standard Contract Standard

Hospital employers customarily fund 100% of claims-made tail insurance if termination occurs without cause or after standard 2-year vesting.
